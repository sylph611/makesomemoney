How to Use

Usage

  1. Enter or paste the text you want to count in the text input field above.
  2. Character count, word count, and other statistics are calculated and displayed in real-time as you type.
  3. You can view both character counts with and without spaces.
  4. Use the 'Clear All' button to remove the text and start over.

Calculation Criteria

  • Total Characters (With Spaces): Count of all characters including spaces and line breaks
  • Characters (Without Spaces): Count of actual characters excluding spaces and line breaks
  • Word Count: Number of words separated by spaces (includes both Korean and English)
  • Korean Characters: Count of Korean syllabic characters
  • English Characters: Count of English alphabetic characters (uppercase and lowercase)
  • Numbers: Count of numeric characters (0-9)

Use Cases

  • Blog/Post Writing: Check appropriate content length
  • Papers/Reports: Verify character limit requirements
  • Social Media Posts: Check platform-specific character limits
  • Resume/Cover Letter: Meet specified character requirements
  • Translation Work: Compare volume between source and translated text
Note: This tool runs in your browser, and your input is never sent to a server. All calculations are processed on your device, ensuring your privacy is protected.
