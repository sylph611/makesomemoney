Unit Converter

Easily convert between various units including length, weight, and temperature. Results are calculated in real-time as you type.

Common Conversions

  • 1 meter = 3.28084 feet
  • 1 kilometer = 0.621371 miles
  • 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters

Common Conversions

  • 1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds
  • 1 pound = 453.592 grams
  • 1 ounce = 28.3495 grams

Reference Temperatures

  • Water freezing point: 0°C = 32°F = 273.15K
  • Water boiling point: 100°C = 212°F = 373.15K
  • Human body temperature: 36.5°C = 97.7°F

How to Use & Conversion Formulas

How to Use

  1. Select a category at the top (Length/Weight/Temperature).
  2. Enter/select the value and original unit to convert.
  3. Select the unit you want to convert to.
  4. The result will be automatically calculated and displayed.
  5. Click the swap button (↓) to switch units.

Length Conversion Formulas

Base Unit: Meter (m)

  • 1 kilometer (km) = 1,000 meters
  • 1 centimeter (cm) = 0.01 meters
  • 1 millimeter (mm) = 0.001 meters
  • 1 mile = 1,609.344 meters
  • 1 yard = 0.9144 meters
  • 1 foot (ft) = 0.3048 meters
  • 1 inch (in) = 0.0254 meters

Weight Conversion Formulas

Base Unit: Gram (g)

  • 1 kilogram (kg) = 1,000 grams
  • 1 milligram (mg) = 0.001 grams
  • 1 ton = 1,000,000 grams
  • 1 pound (lb) = 453.59237 grams
  • 1 ounce (oz) = 28.349523125 grams

Temperature Conversion Formulas

  • Celsius → Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • Fahrenheit → Celsius: °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9
  • Celsius → Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15
  • Kelvin → Celsius: °C = K - 273.15
Note: Conversion results are displayed with up to 6 decimal places, which is the precision commonly used in scientific and engineering applications.
