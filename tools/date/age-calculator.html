Age Calculation Methods

International Age (Korean Age)

The exact age from your date of birth to the present. Your age increases by one year after your birthday passes.

International Age = Current Year - Birth Year (subtract 1 if birthday hasn't passed)

Note: This is also known as "만 나이" (Man-nai) in Korean. As of June 2023, South Korea officially adopted this international age system for all legal and administrative purposes, replacing the traditional Korean age counting methods.

Year Age

The age calculated by subtracting your birth year from the current year. It increases regardless of whether your birthday has passed, changing on January 1st each year.

Year Age = Current Year - Birth Year

Age Calculation Terms

  • International Age: Age calculated precisely from your birth date
  • Year Age: Current year minus birth year
  • Birthday: The annual anniversary of your birth date
  • Elapsed Days: Total number of days from birth to today

Age-Related Systems (South Korea)

  • Official Documents: International age used (since June 2023)
  • School Grade: Those born January 1 - December 31 are in the same grade
  • Military Service: Age 18 or older (international age)
  • Legal Adult: Age 19 (international age, eligible for ID card)
Note: This calculator is based on the Gregorian calendar date of birth. If you have a lunar calendar birthday, please convert it to the Gregorian date first.

Frequently Asked Questions

International age is calculated precisely from your birth date, increasing by one year only after your birthday passes. Year age is calculated by subtracting your birth year from the current year, increasing on January 1st regardless of your birthday.

Example: Someone born on December 31, 2000, would be 23 years old (international age) and 24 years old (year age) on June 1, 2024.

In South Korea, the "Age Unification Law" took effect on June 28, 2023, requiring the use of international age (만 나이) in all official documents and laws. Previously, different age calculation methods were used across various documents and systems, but they are now unified under international age.

However, people can still use year age or traditional Korean age in everyday life.

This calculator uses Gregorian calendar dates for calculations. If you have a lunar calendar birthday, please convert it to the Gregorian date first.

For those born on a lunar leap month, legally the same date in a regular month is considered the birthday. In years with a leap month, the leap month date can be used, and in years without, the regular month date is used.

Legally, age is calculated based on the birth date regardless of birth time. Whether you were born right after midnight or just before midnight, you're considered born on the same day.

However, birth time may be needed when calculating exact survival time.

This calculator is for reference only and has no legal effect. For official documents, please use the date of birth from official documents such as ID cards or family relationship certificates.

For legally important age verification, please contact your local government office or relevant authorities.
