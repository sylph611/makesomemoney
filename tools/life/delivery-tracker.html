Delivery Tracker

Enter your tracking number to be quickly directed to the courier's official tracking page.

📦 How to Use
  1. Select your courier service
  2. Enter your tracking number (numbers only)
  3. Click 'Track Delivery' button to go to the courier's tracking page
e.g., 123456789012
Enter numbers only, without hyphens (-)

    Frequently Asked Questions

    If your tracking number cannot be found, check the following:

    • Just shipped: Tracking becomes available 1-2 hours after registration
    • Number verification: Confirm you entered the tracking number correctly
    • Courier verification: Make sure you selected the correct courier service
    • Remove hyphens: Enter numbers only and remove hyphens (-)
    • Contact customer service: If still not found, contact the courier directly

    This service supports tracking one package at a time. To track multiple packages:

    • Track each tracking number individually
    • Use each courier's official app to manage multiple deliveries simultaneously
    • If purchased from Naver Shopping, Coupang, etc., you can track all packages in their apps

    Delivery information may not update in real-time:

    • Scan points: Information updates only when scanned at courier hubs
    • In transit: No updates while the package is loaded on the delivery vehicle
    • Possible delays: Delivery may be delayed due to high volume, bad weather, or holidays
    • Contact: If no updates for 2-3 days, contact the courier directly

    If you're not home, you can receive your package through:

    • Doorstep delivery: Add "Please leave at door" in delivery instructions
    • Security office: For apartments/offices, can be stored with security
    • Parcel locker: Automatic storage in unmanned parcel lockers if available
    • Redelivery request: Request redelivery using the contact received via SMS
    • In-person pickup: Pick up at the nearest courier branch office
