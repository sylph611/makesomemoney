Understanding BMI

What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is an index that measures obesity using height and weight. It is the obesity assessment standard recommended by the World Health Organization (WHO) and is widely used worldwide.

BMI Calculation Formula

BMI = Weight(kg) ÷ (Height(m) × Height(m))

Example: For a height of 170cm and weight of 70kg
BMI = 70 ÷ (1.7 × 1.7) = 70 ÷ 2.89 = 24.2

BMI Classification Standards (WHO Standards)

Classification BMI Range Health Risk
Underweight Below 18.5 Malnutrition, Weakened immunity
Normal weight 18.5 ~ 24.9 Healthy condition
Overweight 25.0 ~ 29.9 Caution needed
Obese (Class I) 30.0 ~ 34.9 Increased chronic disease risk
Obese (Class II) 35.0 ~ 39.9 High chronic disease risk
Obese (Class III) 40.0 and above Very high risk
⚠️ Important Notes
  • This calculator is for reference only and is not a medical diagnostic tool.
  • BMI does not directly measure body fat percentage, so athletes with high muscle mass may be classified as overweight.
  • Children, adolescents, pregnant women, and elderly people require separate standards.
  • For an accurate health assessment, please consult with healthcare professionals.

Limitations of BMI

  • Does not consider muscle mass: People with high muscle mass may have high BMI.
  • Does not reflect fat distribution: Does not consider the location of fat such as abdominal obesity.
  • Age/Gender differences: The same BMI can have different health effects depending on age and gender.
  • Individual differences: Does not reflect individual characteristics such as skeletal size and body type.

Healthy Weight Management Methods

  • Regular exercise (3-5 times a week, 30 minutes or more)
  • Balanced diet (appropriate ratio of vegetables, protein, and carbohydrates)
  • Adequate sleep (7-8 hours a day)
  • Stress management
  • Regular health checkups
