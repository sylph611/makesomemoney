Privacy Policy

ToolBox values your personal information and complies with all applicable laws and regulations.

Last updated: January 1, 2024

1. Purpose of Use

ToolBox (hereinafter referred to as "the Site") processes personal information for the following purposes. Personal information being processed will not be used for any purpose other than those stated below, and if the purpose of use changes, we will take necessary measures such as obtaining separate consent.

  • Service provision and operation
  • Responding to user inquiries
  • Service improvement and statistical analysis

2. Personal Information We Collect

The Site collects only the minimum personal information necessary to provide the service.

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system information
  • Visit date, time, and usage logs
  • Cookie information

Information Collected During Inquiries

  • Email address (when responding to inquiries)
  • Inquiry content

3. Retention Period

The Site will promptly destroy personal information after the purpose of collection and use has been achieved.

  • Automatically collected information: 1 year from collection date
  • Inquiry records: 3 years after inquiry completion (as per e-commerce laws)

4. Use of Cookies

The Site uses cookies to provide customized services to users.

Purpose of Cookie Use

  • Analyzing user access frequency and visit duration
  • Understanding user preferences and interests
  • Storing user settings such as dark mode
  • Displaying advertisements and providing personalized ads

How to Refuse Cookie Settings

Users can allow all cookies, require confirmation each time a cookie is saved, or refuse all cookies by configuring their web browser options. However, refusing cookies may cause difficulties in using some services.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The Site displays advertisements through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to provide advertisements. For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Ad Settings: https://adssettings.google.com

Google Analytics

The Site may use Google Analytics to analyze website usage. Google Analytics collects information about users' site usage using cookies, and this information is processed anonymously.

6. Third-Party Sharing

The Site does not, in principle, provide users' personal information to external parties. However, exceptions are made in the following cases:

  • When users have given prior consent
  • When required by law or when requested by investigative agencies in accordance with procedures and methods prescribed by law for investigative purposes

7. Security Measures

The Site takes the following measures to ensure the security of personal information:

  • Data encryption transmission through HTTPS protocol
  • Access control management
  • Regular security inspections

8. User Rights

Users may exercise the following rights:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction if there are errors
  • Request deletion
  • Request suspension of processing

You may exercise the above rights through the contact page.

9. Data Protection Officer

The Site has designated a Data Protection Officer who is responsible for overseeing all matters related to personal information processing and handling user complaints and remedying damages related to personal information processing, as follows:

  • Officer: Site Administrator
  • Contact: Please use the Contact page

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is effective from the date of implementation, and if there are additions, deletions, or corrections to the content in accordance with laws and policies, we will announce the changes through the Site at least 7 days before implementation.

11. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding personal information, please contact us through the Contact page.
